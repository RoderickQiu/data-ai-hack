# the right job — 4-minute pitch

Exactly five slides. The demo is included in the four minutes.

## 1. Job search keeps repeating itself

**0:00–0:35 · 35 seconds**

Every new role brings the same work. You check the location, the seniority, and whether the work fits. You repeat facts from your resume. And when a shortlist misses the mark, you explain the same preference again. The time goes into correcting and preparing, before you even get to a conversation. We want the next search to benefit from the decisions you already made.

## 2. the right job

**0:35–0:50 · 15 seconds**

This is the right job: a job-search agent that learns from your decisions. Tell it what matters, correct it when needed, and let that history improve the next shortlist.

## 3. A search that remembers you

**0:50–1:35 · 45 seconds**

The value is a better next search with less repeated work. Start with your resume and preferences. The agent ranks real jobs and explains the fit. When you skip a role, your reason can become a proposed preference, but you confirm it before it changes the ranking. When you choose a role, the application pack reuses known answers and cites verified experience. If a required fact is missing, it asks instead of inventing it. You stay in control of the final application.

## 4. How the sponsor tools work together

**1:35–2:30 · 55 seconds**

Each sponsor owns a specific part of the loop. Hotdata stores and queries the job corpus. Cognee turns resumes and feedback into structured context. HydraDB keeps candidate facts and decision history. RocketRide runs the workflow through our tools. Rote captures successful tool paths and replays the familiar steps; novel work still needs reasoning. Feedback goes back into memory for the next ranking. Snyk checks code and dependencies. In the demo, the proof is the actual ranking, remembered context, and execution trace.

## 5. demo time

**2:30–4:00 · 90 seconds**

Switch to the dashboard. Spend 20 seconds on a shortlist and its match reasons, 25 seconds on feedback and a confirmed preference, 25 seconds on an application pack with reused answers and a flagged missing fact, and 20 seconds on the execution trace and observed run metrics. If the dashboard says “Example numbers, not live yet”, explicitly identify the chart as illustrative. Do not describe its sample curve as a measured result. Close: the right job remembers what matters, so your next search starts further ahead.
